## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Establish a findable presence.** The company is currently invisible online. In a sector where buyers screen for fraud before they screen for price, zero digital footprint is itself a red flag to a prospective customer. A Facebook business page and a Google Business Profile cost nothing and are the minimum.
2. **Lead with title documentation.** For the property arm, publishing the title status of every estate is the single highest-trust action available.
3. **Publish a block spec sheet.** Sizes, mix ratio, cure period, compressive strength, price per unit. Buyers comparing suppliers have nothing else to compare on.
4. **Photograph the plant and the sites.** Proof of physical operation beats claims.
5. **Name the integration.** If the blocks feed the estates, that's the differentiator — build the whole pitch on it.
